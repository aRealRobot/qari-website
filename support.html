Hafez Companion Support

Help with setup, recitation tracking, and troubleshooting

Hafez Companion is an offline Quran recitation companion that listens as you recite — entirely on your device — to follow along, offer hints, and track your progress. If something isn't working as expected, the answers below cover the most common questions. For anything else, reach out directly.

Contact us

Email salatplus25@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and OS version. We read and respond to every message.

Getting started

The first time you open Hafez Companion, it downloads a speech recognition model (used to follow your recitation) and, optionally, an audio pack for Imam+ mode hints. This is a one-time setup — once it completes, the app works fully offline.

  • Keep the app open during setup, on Wi-Fi if possible. The screen can lock; the download continues in the background.
  • Grant microphone access when prompted — Recite, Memorize, and Imam mode all need it to track your recitation. You can change this later in your device's Settings app.

Frequently asked questions

What is Imam mode?

Imam mode is for the person leading the prayer, reciting from memory. It listens as you recite and gives you a quiet hint if you pause too long or seem to lose your place, so you can keep leading with confidence. It also tracks each raka automatically as you move through the prayer.

The app isn't recognizing my recitation. What should I check?

  • Make sure microphone access is granted in your device's Settings.
  • Recite at a normal pace and volume, close to the microphone, in a reasonably quiet space.
  • Confirm the one-time model download finished — a stalled setup download will prevent recitation tracking from working at all.

Does Hafez Companion need an internet connection?

Only for the initial setup download and for downloading Quran audio you haven't played before. Once downloaded, recitation tracking and reading work fully offline.

Does Hafez Companion require an account?

No. There's no sign-in, and the app doesn't ask for your name or email. See the Privacy Policy for details on what the app does and doesn't collect.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Hafez Companion stores lives only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it — there's no account or server-side copy to delete separately.
