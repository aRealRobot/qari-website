Privacy Policy

Hafez Companion Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026 · Applies to the Hafez Companion iOS and Android app

The short version: Hafez Companion listens to your recitation to give you feedback, but that audio is processed entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere. Your practice history stays in a local database on your phone. We don't run ads, trackers, or analytics, and we don't ask you to create an account.

Overview

Hafez Companion is an offline Quran recitation companion. It listens as you recite, using an on-device speech recognition model to follow along, offer hints, and track your progress. This policy explains exactly what that involves from a privacy standpoint, in plain language, followed by the specifics.

Information we collect

Microphone audio

While you're using Recite, Memorize, or Imam mode, Hafez Companion captures audio from your microphone to recognize your recitation in real time. This audio is processed entirely on your device — it is never uploaded, streamed, or transmitted anywhere, and no copy of it is saved after your session ends.

Practice session history

Hafez Companion keeps a local record of your sessions — which surah, how far you got, timing, and any words you needed a hint on — so you can track your own progress over time. This is stored in a database on your device only.

What we don't ask for

Hafez Companion doesn't require a sign-in, and doesn't ask for your name, email address, or any other way to identify you.

Information we don't collect

Analytics or usage tracking

No analytics SDK is included in the app.

Crash reporting

No crash- or error-reporting service is included.

Advertising

No ad network, no advertising identifiers.

Location

Hafez Companion never requests or accesses your location.

Contacts or photos

Hafez Companion doesn't request access to either.

Account or profile data

There's no sign-in, so there's nothing to store about who you are.

How the microphone is used

Hafez Companion's recitation-tracking features (Recite, Memorize, and Imam mode) use a speech recognition model that runs directly on your device to match what you're saying against the expected verse text. This is what lets the app work fully offline, once its one-time setup download is complete, and it's also why your recitation never has to leave your phone: there's no server involved in recognizing your speech.

iOS and Android both require an explicit permission prompt before Hafez Companion can access your microphone. You can review or revoke this at any time in your device's system settings — see Your choices below.

Network connections

Hafez Companion downloads Quran audio and text content, and its recognition model, from the following services the first time you use each feature. These are one-way downloads to your device — none of them receive any personal information, recitation audio, or session history from the app.

Services Hafez Companion connects to, and what each one is for
ServicePurposeWhat it receives
Cloudflare R2 (Hafez Companion's own storage) Hosts the speech recognition model and the downloadable Quran audio pack A standard file-download request. No account or personal data.
everyayah.com Source for Quran audio recitation files A standard file-download request. No account or personal data.
api.quran.com Public Quran.com API — supplies verse text and layout data A standard API request for Quran text. No account or personal data.

Like any request made over the internet, these connections inherently include your device's IP address as part of how networking works — this is not something Hafez Companion adds or uses to identify you, and none of these services are used for tracking or advertising.

Data storage & deletion

Everything Hafez Companion stores — the downloaded recognition model, Quran audio, and your session history — lives in your device's app storage, protected by the same sandboxing iOS and Android apply to every app. None of it is backed up to any server we operate.

Uninstalling Hafez Companion removes all of this data from your device. There's no account to close and no copy of your data anywhere else to delete, because none exists.

Children's privacy

Hafez Companion doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app has no accounts, no analytics, and no data leaves the device, there is no personal information collected from a child user any differently than from an adult user.

Your choices

  • Microphone permission — you can grant, deny, or revoke this at any time in your device's Settings app. Without it, the live recitation-tracking features won't work, but the rest of the app is unaffected.
  • Notifications permission (Android) — used only to show a progress notification while the one-time setup download is running. You can decline this; the download still proceeds, it just won't show a notification.
  • Deleting your data — uninstall the app. Since everything is stored locally, this removes all of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note them in the app's release notes. Continuing to use Hafez Companion after a change means you accept the updated policy.
